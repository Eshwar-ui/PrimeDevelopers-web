# Prime Developer — Brand Guidelines

Source: `PRIME DEVELOPERS brand guidelines.pdf`

## Colors

**Primary palette**
- CG Blue (base): `#0073A4` — RGB (0, 115, 164)
- White (accent): `#FFFFFF`
- Dark Charcoal (neutral): `#2F2F2F`

**Secondary palette**
- Deep Saffron (base): `#FCA42E` — RGB (252, 164, 46)
- Bright Gray (accent): `#EEEEEE`
- Independence (neutral): `#202020`

## Fonts

- **Primary font: Rubik** — humanist sans serif, neutral tone, slightly rounded corners. Weights: Regular, Medium, Bold.
- **Secondary font: Arimo** — high-quality open-source sans-serif (Steve Matteson), strong on-screen readability. Weights: Regular, Italic, Bold.

Both are already wired up in [src/index.css](src/index.css) as `--font-rubik` and `--font-arimo`.

## Logo usage

- Don't stroke, break apart, rotate, flip, shadow, or blur/glow the logo.
- Maintain the safe zone (logomark: 31px exclusion zone; lockup: 29px exclusion zone).
- Minimum placement distance from page corners: 58px (logo), 72px (logomark).
