# Prime Developer Website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The public website serves all visitors evaluating Prime Developer, including prospective tenants, buyers, investors, brokers, partners, and people learning commercial real-estate concepts.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Prime Developer, its Texas property portfolio, expertise, news, and contact paths. It should help visitors understand available opportunities and make informed next steps with the company.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse properties, compare spaces and unit details, learn about the company, read market updates, and submit enquiries. A public learning center explains real-estate terminology in plain language, with one dedicated page and YouTube video per term.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public content and navigation are managed through the existing admin CMS.
- Learning-center terms must be CMS-managed, including their slug, category, definition, explanation, example, related terms, and YouTube URL.
- Missing videos must render an intentional empty state; the product must not invent or substitute video URLs.
- Existing light/dark theming, responsive behavior, route transitions, and accessibility conventions must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Prime Developer. Existing brand assets, typography, palette, and editorial voice in the repository remain authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current website, property data, news content, brand assets, and CMS. No approved educational videos were supplied for the learning center.

## Product Principles

- Explain specialist terminology without assuming prior industry knowledge.
- Connect education to practical property decisions and clear next steps.
- Keep published content editable by the client rather than hard-coded.
- Never fabricate commercial claims, figures, testimonials, or media.
- Make educational content accessible by browsing, search, sharing, and contextual links.
